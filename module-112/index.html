What Module 112 covers

One session for the whole plant: the operator runs every unit of Modules 101–111 from one console — a PCS, an independent ESD and WinCC. Parts I and II of the control-room course, Thu 1 Oct, 10:00–14:00.

  • Describe the PCS / ESD / WinCC architecture and say why the ESD acts independently of the PCS and the HMI.
  • Read a Tendrara HMI screen and its faceplates: state, Auto / Manual, Local / Remote, Simulation and Override.
  • Classify an alarm by priority — Trip, Interlock, Warning, Diagnostic — tell alarm and LSD / PSD / ESD / ASD apart, and find the consequence in the C&E.
  • Change a set point, switch a loop Auto / Manual, start or stop a single drive, respecting permissives — simulation and forcing are not operator tools.
  • Run a console round, explain what goes in the daily and periodic logs, hand over the shift and escalate with the right information.
