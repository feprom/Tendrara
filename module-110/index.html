What Module 110 covers

Four units that make nothing and protect everything — what burns, what detects, what drains, what puts it out. None of them produces LNG; none of the units you have learnt so far can start without them.

  • What burns — four enclosed ground flares in one pit, four headers, pilots always lit, purge always open.
  • What detects — 49 gas, 13 flame and 40 smoke detectors, and which box each one talks to: the ESD or the AM-8200.
  • What drains — every vessel to one buried tank, S-551, and its two pumps; nothing drains without flare and nitrogen first.
  • What puts it out — the fire-water ring, two diesel pumps and a jockey on a 7 / 6.5 / 5 barg ladder, foam on LNG, water only to cool.
  • When it happens — the cause-and-effect matrix, the four shutdown sets, blowdown to the flare, and what the deck does not know.

Lighting a pilot and proving it: START, spark, 200 °C, FLAME ON

Six steps from the pilot-gas check to the green lamp. A pilot is not «on» because the spark fired: it is on when the thermocouples prove it.

1
operator

Check the pilot-gas pressure is adequate: the PSL indicator is OFF (yellow lamp off). A lit lamp means PSL-23x1 sees low pressure — do not start.

E2022034_H01 rev 1 p.33
2
operator

Set selector 6SF1 to LOCAL (front pushbuttons) or REMOTE (24 VDC commands from the customer terminal block).

E2022034_H01 rev 1 p.32–33
3
operator → panel

Press START (local) or send START (remote): ignition relay KS pulls in and the two high-energy modules 5HE1 and 5HE2 are activated at the same time.

E2022034_H01 rev 1 p.33
4
panel

Continuous spark train at both pilot tips through the armoured HV cables: 2 sparks/s, 18 J each.

E2022034_H01 rev 1 p.33
5
flame proving

Pilot gas ignites; TE-23x1A / TE-23x1B rise above the set-point (typically > 200 °C) → K109TC 4–20 mA → Z113S alarm generators confirm flame → KTC1 / KTC2 energise → FLAME ON green lamp.

E2022034_H01 rev 1 p.33
6
panel

The ignition command is removed automatically after the RE22R1MKNR turn-off delay, to limit the duty cycle of the ignition modules. The pilots stay lit on gas alone.

Block 3 — Unit 550 - Closed drain

Toxic, flammable, pressurised: what opening a drain means

Opening a drain releases toxic, flammable and pressurised fluid: depressurise and N2-wash first, and never drain what the philosophy says must be vented.

Before you open
Depressurise first
  • Depressurise to atmospheric, then N2 vapor-wash [ITF note 1 rev (07-2026) p.n/a]
  • Reason: avoid toxic or HC vapour release on opening [ITF note 1 rev (07-2026) p.n/a]
Cold drains
Ice blocks headers
  • Liquids below 0 °C from adiabatic depressurisation [SAF03-ING-FR01 rev 2 p.7]
  • Ice from freezing water blocks the header and can breach it [SAF03-ING-FR01 rev 2 p.7]
  • Mitigation: header segregated below-0 °C vs above-0 °C [SAF03-ING-FR01 rev 2 p.7]
Liquid HC spill
Curbs and pits
  • Liquid-HC area: concrete platform, curbs, recovery pit [SAF03-ING-FR01 rev 2 p.3]
  • Portable pump and recovery drum for the pit [SAF03-ING-FR01 rev 2 p.3]
  • LNG impoundment: 110 % of max liquid capacity, NFPA 59A [SAF03-ING-FR01 rev 2 p.11]
Confined space
Break containment
  • Entry: spools removed, or spades/blinds on all connections [SAF01-ING-FR01 rev 2 p.39]
  • Relief valves off, blind flange on the flare-header opening [SAF01-ING-FR01 rev 2 p.39]
Ammonia and LNG
Vent, never drain
  • Ammonia: vented to the ammonia flare header if required [SAF03-ING-FR01 rev 2 p.11]
  • Ammonia must never mix with process gas [SAF03-ING-FR01 rev 2 p.11]
  • Cryogenic LNG: emptied by venting, not drained [SAF03-ING-FR01 rev 2 p.11]

Blowdown to the flare: who initiates it, why the BDV fails open, what the headers must take

1
Detection

The fire detection system or the smoke detection system activates the emergency shutdown sequence (ESD) automatically; the blowdown system is activated in case of gas or fire detection.

SAF05-ING-FR01 rev 1 p.19, p.4
2
Manual initiation

Operators initiate ESD by the pushbutton on the Safety Panel or by one of the several pushbuttons of the E.S.D. circuit along the perimeter of the installation.

SAF05-ING-FR01 rev 1 p.19
3
Depressurization

After ESD activation, emergency depressurization of the fire zone or of the whole facility (small installation) is executed manually or automatically.

SAF05-ING-FR01 rev 1 p.19
4
Every BDV at once

All depressurizing valves within the fire zone or the installation are de-energized simultaneously: de-energized means open, because the BDV must fail open.

SAF05-ING-FR01 rev 1 p.19
5
Into the collection system

Each blowdown device (fail-open pneumatic valve + restriction orifice sized for the required rate) discharges into the appropriate flare collection system, the same one the overpressure and safety valves discharge to.

SAF05-ING-FR01 rev 1 p.19, p.21
6
The flare receives it

All four pilot flames continuously lit, vent gas routed automatically to the corresponding flare manifold and combusted; the headers are kept on purge.
