Scope · Objectives · Agenda

What Module 102 covers

Objectives — by the end you can:

  • Explain why CO₂ and water must leave the gas before the cold box (freeze-out · corrosion · hydrates).
  • Trace the U330 gas path (E-331 · V-331 · T-331) and the amine loop (V-332 · E-332 · T-332/E-333 · E-334 · filters · P-332A/B).
  • Describe the MDEA absorption/regeneration principle and what foaming does to it.
  • Trace the U310 gas path (E-311 · V-311 · T-311A/B · dust filters · mercury guards) and the TSA regeneration circuit (X-312 · E-313 · V-312).
  • Name the control loops of both units and their final elements.
  • Recognize the critical alarms & trips — including AAHH-3301 (CO₂) and AAHH-3101 (moisture), the two product-quality PSDs.
  • Read the U330 / U310 HMI screens and locate the unit documents.
  • Carry out the routine field checks: filter ΔP, bed ΔP (local gauges), amine levels & make-up, fan rows.

Key messages — Module 102

  • 1 · Why these units exist: CO₂ freezes · water blocks · mercury corrodes — treatment protects the cold box.
  • 2 · U330 gas path: E-331 → V-331 → T-331 → (E-331) → U310 · amine loop: T-331 → V-332 → E-332 → T-332/E-333 → E-332 → P-332A/B → E-334 → filters → FV-3301 → T-331.
  • 3 · U310 gas path: E-311 → V-311 → T-311A/B → F-312A/B → T-312A/B → SDV-3560 → U350 · regen: BOG → X-312 → bed → E-313 → V-312 → F-311A/B → back.
  • 4 · The two product-quality trips: AAHH-3301 (CO₂) · AAHH-3101 (moisture, ppm) — both PSD.
  • 5 · Numbers to know cold: ≤10 %mol CO₂ in · 4.5 barg flash · 5 barg regenerator · 6 h + 6 h TSA · 232–235 °C · 3 bar/min.
  • 6 · LL level = stop the pump / close the outlet · HH level or pressure = PSD — same gas blow-by logic as Unit 200.
  • 7 · References: Operating Manual Ch. 5 & 6 · SAF01 · SAF05 · SAF12 · P&IDs PR02–PR10.
