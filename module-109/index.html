What Module 109 covers

One small area that the rest of the plant leans on: four heat exchangers in three other units all wait on this one loop. Two fired heaters, two loops, an expansion tank under a nitrogen blanket, and one burner that can take three different fuels.

  • Start with who depends on it — the four users, and the hazards of oil at 180 °C.
  • Follow the circuit on one drawing: primary loop to the heaters, secondary loop to the users.
  • Open the heater. TPC 6000B, its burner and its two trains — and why flow comes before fire.
  • Then the fuels — gas, condensate and diesel to the same burner — and what stops them.
  • Finish with operating it: filling and venting, start and stop, upsets and maintenance.

Block 1 · before any operating detail — Unit 410 · hot oil · furnace, expansion vessel, three fuels

The hazards of oil at 180 °C

Six risks on this unit: thermal oil at 180 °C, a nitrogen-blanketed vessel, three flammable fuels, hot flue gas, an uninsulated furnace wall, and the standing lock-out requirement.

1 · thermal oil, hot
180 °C
  • Outlet at 180 °C in all three duty cases (PFD p1)
  • Up to 6.62 barg at the furnace inlet (PFD p1)
  • Contact burns on any uninsulated line or fitting
2 · expansion vessel & N₂
3.5 BARG N₂
  • V-412 rated 6 bar(g) / 300 °C design (offer §1.9 p13)
  • Nitrogen blanket at 3.5 barg (offer §1.9 p13; PR28 rev 10)
  • N₂ purge → asphyxiation risk in an enclosed space
3 · three flammable fuels
3 FUELS
  • Diesel oil · condensate F109 at 7.5 barg
  • Fuel gas at 0.495 barg (PFD p1; offer p9)
  • All three feed the same unit — leak plus ignition source
4 · hot flue gas
204 °C
  • Flue gas at 204 °C (PFD p1)
  • Duct and stack run at flue-gas temperature
  • Contact burns on duct and stack surfaces
5 · uninsulated furnace wall
AIR-COOLED WALL
  • Wall is air-cooled, not insulated (offer p7, p4)
  • “Insulation material: N.A.” per the offer (offer p7)
  • Hot surface over the full wall area
6 · LOTO & area response
LOTO
  • Lock-out / tag-out before any intervention
  • Extinguishers 410-153 / 410-166 in the area
  • Spillage kit 410-171 in the area
No intervention on this unit without lock‑out / tag‑out.extinguishers 410-153 / 410-166 · spillage kit 410-171 · SAF07-ING-PR02 rev 11

Key messages — Module 109

  1. 1The oil always leaves at 180 °C— what changes with load is the return: 128 / 145,5 / 164,4 °C.PFD rev 0

  3. 3Circulation first, then flame— without flow the coil cooks; a differential switch protects it.offer · hextherm jul-26
  4. 4Three fuels, one burner— diesel to start and pilot, condensate F109 as the main fuel, gas as integration.offer §1.4
