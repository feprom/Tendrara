What Module 109 covers

One area, and it is the power plant of the plant: everything you have learnt in the previous modules runs on what comes out of here. Seven gas gensets, two diesel gensets, four busbars at 690 V and a master that decides how many machines run — in permanent island mode.

  • Start with the numbers — what the plant asks for and what is installed: five running, two in reserve.
  • Follow the fuel gas on one drawing: two headers, not one, and fourteen orifice plates.
  • Open the machine. A lean-burn engine with a prechamber, its gas train, its water, its oil, its batteries.
  • Then safety — gas and fire in a closed container, and the cause-and-effect matrix.
  • Finish with maintenance: three clocks, and what the operator may touch without calling anyone.

The hazards of this unit

Six risks per gas engine module: fuel gas at 1–8 barg, 690 V generation, exhaust at 385 °C, 106 dBA, CO in a gas-tight container, 1,500 rpm and diesel storage.

1 · gas in a closed container
1–8 BARG
  • Module gas piping at 1–8 barg inside a sealed container
  • 20% LEL → prealarm + module stop · 40% LEL → alarm
  • Two gas detectors JI-100 / JI-101 inside the container
2 · electrical
690 V · 85 kA
  • Four 690 V TN-C busbars, breaking capacity Icu 85 kA
  • Breaker +GENCB 2,500 A — only the engine control closes it
  • 24 V AGM batteries also feed the Master Control, diode-decoupled
3 · exhaust heat
385 °C
  • 10,118 kg/h of exhaust gas through a DN600 duct
  • Uninsulated sections: contact and radiant burns
  • Exhaust through silencer 13-SIL-1 to the stack
4 · noise
106 dBA
  • Dry cooler fans 11-AER-1 rated LWA 106 dBA
  • Silencer 13-SIL-1 takes the exhaust down to 72 dB(A)
  • Hearing protection near the container and the cooler
5 · CO & confined space
GAS-TIGHT BOX
  • The module container is a sealed, gas-tight enclosure
  • Purge fan + damper run after every stop and every failed start
  • Re-entry only with a verified gas-clear condition
6 · rotating parts, fuel & LOTO
1,500 RPM
  • Engine and alternator at 1,500 rpm
  • Diesel day tank 1,000 L over a containment bund
  • Lock-out / tag-out per TA 2300-0010 before any intervention

alarms · block 4 — What the machine does, class by class

When something fails: four classes, four actions

INNIO does not classify by how bad the symptom looks. It classifies by what the controller makes the machine do. The class sets the response — the operator does not choose it.

Trigger
What the machine does
What the operator does
P1Emergency stop
Any Category I/II trip condition or manual E-stop.
Ignition OFF, generator breaker OPEN, gas solenoid valves CLOSED — all at once, with whatever load is on the machine. No unload step first.
Fix the cause before restart (TA 1100-0111). Category I/II faults (oil pressure, overspeed, knock, deflagration, main-bearing temperature, and more) call INNIO or the service partner before reset.
P2Automatic protective trip
A protection function reaches its limit (e.g. prechamber gas pressure low, charge temperature max).
Unloads toward <10% of rated power first; the breaker opens at that point — or 1 s later at the latest, whichever comes first. Gas valves close. Not an instant break like P1.
Same rule: fix the cause before restart. Remote reset is capped at 5 times per 6 h of load operation, by the customer's chosen risk category (0–4).
P3Normal stop
Request off — an operator-ordered stop or a fault the controller treats as a normal-stop case (e.g. 3rd failed start).
Runs the SAME program as an operator stop: unload, breaker open <10% Pn, ignition off below starting speed, exhaust purge, gas-rail leak test, then 5 min of auxiliaries after the engine stops. Not a shutdown — the request is withdrawn.
No fault to clear for a routine request-off. A fault routed to P3 (e.g. start-up failure) still needs its cause found before the next start.
P4Warning
A condition outside normal but not yet at a protective limit.
Signalled to the operator only. The module keeps running — no automatic action on load or breaker.
Acknowledge and fix the cause before the warning re-arms (same OM p188/p707 rule as any other alarm).
P2 unloads toward <10% Pn before the breaker opens (at that point, or 1 s later at the latest) — it is not an instant break like P1. P3 is a request withdrawn, not a shutdown: it runs the same program an operator stop would run. Knocking and misfire both cut power to 50% Pn before tripping. Remote reset of a trip fault is capped at 5 times per 6 h of load operation. Source: AL-SO59108_BL63 p3 · TSOC mod p16, p27–p28 · OM p188–p191, p707.

Block 6 · gas and fire response — Area 480 · module container · P.17 loop

Gas and fire inside the container

Fire and gas alarms pull in opposite directions — fire confines, gas ventilates. When both fire together, gas has priority over fire (TSOC mod p35).

FIRE — confine
WHOLE PLANT STOPS
  • Detectors P.17-JI-210 / 211 / 212 (smoke)
  • Trip: emergency shutdown of the whole installation
  • Louvres CLOSE, circulation fans STOP
GAS — ventilate
THIS MODULE STOPS
  • Detectors P.17-JI-100 / 101, thresholds 20% / 40% LEL
  • Trip: this module stops
  • Louvres stay OPEN, fans to MAXIMUM speed
Together: gas wins. Ventilate before you try to confine anything — the fire response (close, stop) is overridden by the gas response (open, max fans) (TSOC mod p35; OM p329).
