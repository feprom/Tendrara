Scope · Objectives · Agenda

What Module 101 covers

Objectives — by the end you can:

  • Describe the function and flow path of Unit 200 — from SDV-2024 to Unit 330.
  • Identify the main equipment: V-202, E-201, F-201A/B, V-201.
  • Explain the three conditioning duties: separation · heating (hydrate prevention) · filtration.
  • Name the control loops and their set points (FIC · PIC · TIC · LIC).
  • Recognize the critical alarms & trips and what each one shuts down.
  • Read the Unit 200 HMI screens and locate the unit documents.
  • Carry out the routine field checks & maintenance: filter ΔP swap-over, hydrate-temperature watch, inhibitor tote & separator-level rounds.
  • Trace the water Unit 200 separates through Unit 530 — degasser, coalescer, two filter stages — and say where the oil and the water each end up.

Operation · Rounds

Daily checks on Unit 200

  • Filter ΔP (PDG-2001) — trend it; swap duty/standby & schedule cartridge change before the alarm.
  • E-201 outlet temperature (TT-2002 vs TIC-2002 SP) — the hydrate watch, critical on winter nights.
  • Corrosion inhibitor XX-201 — confirm injection & tote level.
  • Separator levels V-202 / V-201 steady — watch for water carryover or interface drift.
  • Skid walk: no leaks, no icing, no passing valves · PSV/BDV lines unobstructed.
  • CK checkpoints + shift log — record every reading and anomaly.
Items derived from the unit design (Ch. 3) — the formal round checklist follows the operating procedures.
Take-home · Recap

Key messages — Module 101

  • 1 · Flow path: SDV-2024 → V-202 → E-201 → PV-2001 → F-201A/B → V-201 → FV-2001 → U330.
  • 2 · Three duties: separate (2 stages) · heat (hydrate prevention) · filter (5 µm).
  • 3 · Loops to know cold: FIC-2001 @ 16.0 MMSCFD (throughput) · TIC-2002 (hydrate defense) · PIC-2001 @ 45.0 barg.
  • 4 · Trips: HH → PSD · LL level → LSD closes that phase outlet — prevents gas blow-by.
  • 5 · Nothing wasted: water → U530 · condensates → S-413 boiler fuel · bypass → E-371 fuel gas.
  • 6 · References: Operating Manual Ch. 3 · SAF01 · SAF05 · SAF12 · HMI Philosophy.
