§1The five states of the block — the three-second test

Same composition, five data rows. Running: state ring solid in the machine's series colour, load gauge filled to its share of the nameplate, P at 10.5 px bold. Stopped: thin grey ring, gauge track present and empty — a measured zero. No measure: dashed grey ring and no gauge at all, because an empty gauge would read as 0 %. Stale: past 12 minutes the whole figure turns grey and the age turns amber. Gap: the figure disappears and the block says since when. The last two rows and the cont. energy label are synthetic, to show the marks — no measurement is stale or in gap in the plant right now; everything in §2 is real.
